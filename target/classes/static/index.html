Key Features

Tailored for the Entire Campus Community

Everything needed to turn chaotic informal complaints into streamlined facility management.

For Students

Report issues in seconds with photo evidence, choose location and category, track live timeline updates, and give feedback upon resolution.

  • Photo evidence attachment
  • 4-step real-time tracker
  • 5-star rating & review

For Administrators

Executive dashboard with campus-wide analytics, technician workload monitoring, ticket assignment, and category/location management.

  • Live Chart.js analytics
  • Workload balancing
  • Campus master configs

For Technicians

Personalized work order queue prioritized by urgency. One-click status advancement with repair documentation and resolution notes.

  • Criticality priority sorting
  • Repair logs & notes
  • Completed task history
